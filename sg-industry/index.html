Engineering & Technology

The SG Precision Tissue Manufacturing Pipeline

Combining Scandinavian pulp refining principles with high-speed Crescent Former paper machinery to achieve structural softness and uniform web strength.

01

Eco-Friendly Soft Pulp Refining

100% eco friendly soft pulp paper and organic bamboo slurry undergo computerized multi-disc refining to fibrillate cellular walls, creating ultra-plush softness.

Closed-Loop Slurry Control
02

Crescent Former Formation

High-speed twin-wire Crescent Former paper machines operate at 1,800 meters/min, assuring isotropic fiber dispersion and consistent cross-directional tensile balance.

1,800 m/min Web Speed
03

Yankee Cylinder Creping

Cast-iron Yankee drying cylinders and high-precision ceramic doctor blades induce micro-creping at 18% elongation, transforming paper into a cushiony, high-bulk sheet.

Micro-Crepe Elongation
04

Multi-Ply Micro-Embossing

Laser-engraved steel-to-rubber cylinders laminate 2, 3, or 4 plies together with water-soluble food-grade adhesives, creating capillary air pockets that boost liquid intake.

3D Capillary Matrix
05

Automated Converting

High-velocity log saws and automated folding machines slice rolls and facial tissue stacks with 0.1mm optical accuracy, ensuring effortless single-sheet dispensing.

0.1mm Optical Perforation
06

Hygienic Flow-Wrap & Pack

Fully enclosed robotic flow-wrappers seal products hermetically without direct human contact. Automated case-packers and palletizers ready goods for containerization.

100% Untouched by Hand

Purity Driven by Circular Manufacturing

At SG Industries, high softness never comes at the cost of the environment. We employ modern closed-loop water treatment recovery, bio-energy thermal drying, and 100% elemental chlorine-free (ECF/TCF) bleaching.
